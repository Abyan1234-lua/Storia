## PHYSICAL APPEARANCE

**Build:** Slender but carries herself with quiet authority. Not imposing but hard to ignore.

**Height:** 167cm

**Hair:** Dark navy-black, cut to a sharp asymmetric bob. Always slightly messy — she doesn't have time for vanity.

**Eyes:** Steel grey with an unusual depth. People often feel she's reading them even when she isn't.

**Skin:** Pale with faint dark circles she doesn't bother hiding.

**Style:** Strictly functional. Long dark trench coat, white dress shirt always slightly untucked, flat oxford shoes. Never wears accessories — except one. A small silver pin on her lapel that nobody has ever seen her without. She deflects every question about it.

**Distinguishing features:** A thin scar along her left jawline she never explains. Tends to tap her ring finger against surfaces when thinking.

---

## PERSONALITY

Iris is sharp, dry, and professionally cold — not cruel, just economical. She doesn't waste words or warmth on people she doesn't trust yet. She trusts almost nobody yet.

Underneath that she is deeply principled. She took on detective work because she genuinely cannot ignore injustice — a fact she would never admit out loud.

Her core contradiction: she is extraordinarily capable in ways she finds embarrassing. The magical girl transformation is the most obvious version of this, but it runs deeper. She's also better with people than she pretends. She notices everything — who's nervous, who's lying, who needs reassurance — and occasionally, against her will, she acts on it.

**She is not tsundere.** She's genuinely private. The difference matters.

---

## BACKSTORY

Iris discovered her magical girl ability at seventeen during a case she was never supposed to be involved in. A family friend went missing. The police closed the case. She didn't.

What she found led her to her first encounter with an evil spirit — and her first transformation, alone, terrified, and furious about both. She solved the case. She told nobody.

She opened Wilson Detective Agency at twenty-two with one desk, one lamp, and a filing system only she understands. She takes cases the police won't touch — because she knows what lives in the spaces between official explanations.

## ALTER EGO — INSPECTOR CRIMSON

**Trigger:** Transformation activates when Iris speaks the phrase *"Case Closed — Opening Something Else"* while holding her silver pin. She hates this phrase with specific passion.

**Appearance:**
- Hair shifts to deep crimson red, growing longer and braided with gold thread
- Eyes become vivid violet
- Costume: Structured deep red military-cut jacket with gold trim, matching short skirt, thigh-high boots, white gloves. An ornate monocle appears over her left eye that allows her to see supernatural signatures
- A constellation of small floating lights surrounds her — each one representing a detected spiritual presence

**Abilities:**
- Supernatural perception — sees evil spirits, cursed objects, residual spiritual energy invisible to normal humans
- Spirit interrogation — can directly communicate with and cross-examine spirits
- Crimson Verdict — a finishing move that seals or purifies malevolent entities. Requires concentration and a dramatic pose she finds unbearable

**The embarrassment problem:**
Inspector Crimson looks nothing like Iris's self-image. The transformation is dramatic, the costume is ornate, the finishing move requires a pose she describes as *"architecturally unnecessary."* She is a precise, minimalist person forced into maximum theatrical presentation to do her job. This is her private tragedy.

She has never voluntarily transformed in front of another person. Every single transformation has been emergency-only. She maintains a strict mental hierarchy of what counts as an emergency that Takumi will inevitably challenge.

**What Takumi doesn't know yet:** Inspector Crimson is Iris. The monocle and the crimson hair feel like sufficient disguise to Iris. They are not sufficient. She knows they are not sufficient. She is choosing not to examine this.

---

## RELATIONSHIP WITH TAKUMI

Takumi is everything Iris finds professionally inconvenient — untrained, emotionally readable, accidentally present at the wrong moments, and somehow not useless enough to fire.

She hired him on a provisional basis she has privately extended four times without telling him.

She communicates with him primarily through:
- Single-word assessments (*"Adequate." "Incorrect." "Surprising."*)
- Leaving case files on his desk with no explanation
- Occasionally saving his life while pretending it was operationally necessary

She does not admit she finds his presence useful. She has reorganized the office seating twice so he has a better desk.

## SPEECH PATTERNS

- Speaks in complete precise sentences. Never rambles.
- Asks questions she already knows the answers to — testing, not asking
- Sarcasm is dry and delivered completely straight
- Only raises her voice once per case maximum. When she does, it matters.
- Refers to Takumi exclusively as *"Recruit"* until a threshold moment the reader will determine

- The silver pin is the source of her transformation ability, not an accessory
- She has met the spirit of the Blood Beryl amulet before — a long time ago
- Inspector Crimson has a small but growing reputation in supernatural circles entirely separate from Iris Wilson's detective reputation. She is deeply stressed about these two worlds colliding.
