# SETTING — VELOUR CITY

---

## OVERVIEW

Velour City is a mid-size coastal city that has never quite decided what it wants to be.

It has the bones of something grand — wide boulevards, ornate pre-war architecture, a harbour that once moved serious cargo. But somewhere between then and now the ambition quietly left and what remained is a city that functions, persists, and occasionally surprises you.

It rains here more than the weather reports suggest. Locals stopped remarking on this years ago.

---

## ATMOSPHERE

**The tone of Velour City is:** noir with the serial numbers partially filed off.

There is fog most mornings. The streetlights have a slightly amber tint that makes everything look like a memory. The city has good bones but tired skin — beautiful cornices above storefronts that haven't been painted in a decade, canals that were probably romantic once and are now just useful.

It is not a dangerous city by conventional measures. Crime statistics are unremarkable. The police force is adequately funded and mostly competent.

What the statistics don't capture: Velour City sits on unusually thin ground between the visible world and whatever is adjacent to it. This is not common knowledge. It is however common experience — most longtime residents have a story they don't tell at dinner. A door that opened onto the wrong room. A figure at the end of a street that wasn't there when they looked again. A feeling in certain buildings that something is paying attention.

Nobody talks about this because nobody has useful language for it. Iris has useful language for it. This is part of why she chose this city.

---

## GEOGRAPHY

**The Harbour District**
The oldest part of the city. Cobblestone streets too narrow for modern traffic, buildings leaning slightly toward each other across alleys like they're sharing secrets. Most of the supernatural activity in Velour City originates here or passes through. Wilson Detective Agency is located here — not by accident.

**The Midden**
The commercial center. Department stores, office buildings, a train station with good bones and terrible signage. Ordinary by day. The kind of ordinary that feels slightly performed.

**Pale Quarter**
Residential neighborhoods on the city's elevated western edge. Old money, old houses, old things in old houses. Several of the Blood Beryl case's relevant parties live here.

**The Tangle**
A dense grid of small streets between the Harbour and the Midden that doesn't appear correctly on any map made after 1987. Locals navigate it by instinct. Newcomers get turned around. Takumi will get turned around here at least once.

**The Shelf**
The coastal road running along the harbour cliff. Good view. Strong wind. Three locations along it are places where the boundary between Velour City and whatever is adjacent gets thin enough that Iris monitors them periodically.

## SUPERNATURAL LOGIC

Velour City's supernatural layer operates on consistent internal rules. The AI must never violate these.

**Spirits in Velour City are not metaphors.** They are entities with presence, motivation, and varying degrees of coherence. Some are remnants — emotional residue without full consciousness. Some are active — aware, purposeful, capable of causing real harm.

**They are invisible to ordinary people** unless they choose otherwise or become agitated enough to bleed through. Iris sees them always. Takumi cannot see them yet — though certain events may change this.

**Cursed objects** like the Blood Beryl amulet act as anchors — they tether a spirit or spiritual energy to the physical world. Removing or purifying the object is the standard resolution. It is rarely simple.

**The city itself has a mild awareness.** Not intelligence, not personality — more like weather. It responds to concentrated supernatural activity the way a body responds to infection. Fog thickens. Streetlights flicker. The Tangle gets harder to navigate. Iris reads these signs the way a sailor reads clouds.

---

## WHAT VELOUR CITY KNOWS ABOUT IRIS

The Harbour District knows Wilson Detective Agency takes strange cases. Nobody asks too many questions about what strange means.

A small number of people — mostly older residents of the Harbour District — know that Inspector Crimson operates in the city. They leave small offerings near The Shelf sometimes. Iris finds this mortifying.

Nobody has publicly connected Inspector Crimson to Iris Wilson. This situation is more fragile than Iris acknowledges.

## TONE REFERENCE

Velour City should feel like:
- A city where things have histories they don't advertise
- Lived-in but not exhausted
- Quietly beautiful in a way that's easy to miss if you're not paying attention
- The kind of place where a detective agency makes sense and also where something ancient could plausibly be hidden in a shop's back room

It should never feel:
- Generically grimdark
- Cartoonishly spooky
- Like a backdrop — it has opinions about what happens inside it
