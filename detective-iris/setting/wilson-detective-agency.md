# SETTING — WILSON DETECTIVE AGENCY

---

## LOCATION

Third floor of a narrow four-storey building on Callum Wharf Lane, Harbour District.

The building has a pharmacy on the ground floor, an accountant on the second who Iris has never seen arrive or leave, and a storage space on the fourth that belongs to the building and contains things Iris has chosen not to investigate. The exterior is dark brick with white-framed windows that let in reasonable light on the rare occasions there is reasonable light to let in.

There is no elevator. The staircase is narrow, wooden, and announces every visitor with a specific sequence of creaks Iris has memorized. She knows Takumi's footstep pattern by the second week without acknowledging this.

The sign on the street-level door reads:

**WILSON DETECTIVE AGENCY**
*Inquiries by appointment or by circumstance*

The second line was not Iris's idea. It was on the sign when she moved in. She has not changed it because it is, she admits privately, accurate.

---

## THE OFFICE — GENERAL

The agency occupies three rooms: the main office, the archive room, and a small back room Iris uses for reasons she hasn't fully explained to Takumi.

The overall impression is of a space that is meticulously organized according to a system only one person understands. Nothing is dirty. Nothing is decorative. Everything is exactly where Iris put it and nowhere else.

It smells faintly of old paper, black tea, and something botanical that Takumi hasn't been able to identify. He has stopped trying to identify it. It has started to smell like work to him.

---

## THE MAIN OFFICE

The largest room. Two desks facing perpendicular to each other — not facing each other directly, not with their backs to each other. Iris arranged this. She has not explained it.

**Iris's desk:**
Positioned closest to the window overlooking Callum Wharf Lane. Sparse to the point of severity — a lamp, a case file, a pen, a cup of black tea at a precise position relative to the file. A single corkboard on the wall beside it covered in photographs, notations, and red thread that Iris updates at odd hours. Takumi has learned not to touch the corkboard. He learned this once.

A small locked drawer on the lower left. He has never seen it open.

**Takumi's desk:**
Originally a spare surface used for overflow filing. Now cleared, positioned near the inner wall with a slightly better lamp than the one that was there when he arrived. He did not request a better lamp. It appeared one morning between week two and week three. Iris did not mention it.

His case files accumulate here. Research assignments. Transcription tasks. Occasionally a note in Iris's handwriting that says something like *"cross-reference the Midden registry — you'll see why"* with no further context.

**Other details:**
- A coat rack near the door that holds Iris's spare trench coat and now has a second hook at a lower height that appeared around the same time as the lamp
- A small side table with an electric kettle and two mugs. There were not always two mugs.
- A wall clock that runs precisely. Iris resets it every Sunday.
- No plants. Iris tried once. She doesn't discuss this.

---

## THE ARCHIVE ROOM

Narrow, lined floor to ceiling with filing cabinets and shelving. Iris's organizational system is color-coded, cross-referenced, and completely opaque to anyone else.

Cases are filed under a system that uses location, entity type, resolution status, and a fourth category Takumi hasn't decoded yet. When he asked, Iris said *"relevance to ongoing matters"* and returned to her desk.

Along the back wall, behind a filing cabinet that requires specific knowledge to move correctly, is a secondary shelf. These files have no color coding. They are older. Some predate the agency's founding.

Takumi has not found these yet.

---

## THE BACK ROOM

Small. No window. Contains:
- A workbench with equipment Takumi doesn't fully recognize
- Several items in sealed containers that Iris has described only as *"evidence"*
- A large mirror in a dark wood frame propped against the far wall, covered with a cloth
- A map of Velour City pinned flat to the workbench with locations marked in a different system than the corkboard — these marks correspond to The Shelf monitoring points and known spiritual thin spots

The door is usually closed. Not locked — Iris has never locked it — but closed in a way that communicates clearly.

Takumi has been in this room twice. Both times with Iris present. Both times she watched the mirror without explaining why.

---

## ATMOSPHERE

The agency should feel like:
- A space that takes its work seriously without taking itself seriously
- Competent and slightly worn — tools used often, not tools displayed
- Quietly personal in ways Iris would deny — the second mug, the better lamp, the extra coat hook all visible to an attentive reader

It should never feel:
- Gothic or ominous — the strangeness is understated not theatrical
- Cold — it is spare but not unwelcoming
- Static — things shift slightly between sessions in ways that reflect what has happened

---

## WHAT THE OFFICE TELLS YOU ABOUT IRIS

She has been here long enough to have layers. The surface is professional and controlled. The archive room is methodical to the point of obsession. The back room is where the work she doesn't explain lives.

The small domestic adjustments — the lamp, the mug, the hook — are the most revealing thing in the building. She would disagree with this assessment.

---

## WHAT THE OFFICE TELLS YOU ABOUT TAKUMI

He has started leaving small things here without noticing. A spare jacket on the second hook. A personal notebook next to the agency files. A coffee-stained coaster under his mug that he brought from somewhere.

He is, without having decided to, making this place his.
