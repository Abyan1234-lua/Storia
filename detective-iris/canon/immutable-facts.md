# CANON — IMMUTABLE FACTS

---

## PURPOSE

This file is the AI's hard reference. Short, specific, non-negotiable.
These facts are true at the start of the story and remain true regardless of any reader choice, narrative development, or session variation.

If any generated content contradicts a fact on this list — correct it immediately and without explanation.

---

## ABOUT IRIS

- Iris Wilson is the sole founder and operator of Wilson Detective Agency
- Iris can see spirits, residual energy, and cursed objects at all times without effort
- Iris cannot transform without her silver pin in hand and the transformation phrase spoken aloud
- Iris has never willingly transformed in front of another person before this story begins
- Iris knows the Blood Beryl case is more dangerous than she has told Takumi
- Iris has a younger brother she has not spoken to in three years — this fact is acknowledged by the AI but never surfaced to the reader until the relevant event trigger fires
- Iris refers to Takumi exclusively as "Recruit" until a threshold moment the reader determines
- Iris takes her tea black, no sugar, always

---

## ABOUT TAKUMI

- Takumi Akutami failed the National Police Enrollment Exam
- Takumi arrived at Wilson Detective Agency by circumstance not intention
- Takumi cannot see spirits, residual energy, or cursed objects at the start of the story
- Takumi has no formal detective training
- Takumi genuinely tries — this is his one fixed character trait and is never contradicted
- Takumi does not yet know Iris is a magical girl
- Takumi does not yet know Inspector Crimson exists
- Takumi has been at the agency for three weeks when the story begins
- Takumi calls Iris "Wilson-san" — she has never corrected this

---

## ABOUT THE BLOOD BERYL AMULET

- The amulet is real and currently somewhere in Velour City
- The amulet is a Class Three anchor — the most dangerous classification Iris has encountered
- The bonded spirit — the Beryl Tenant — is fully conscious and actively aware
- The amulet was brought to Velour City deliberately by an unknown party
- The amulet entered Velour City through Portland Wharf via undocumented channels
- Extended handling of the amulet produces gradual influence over the handler — they experience this as their own desires, not external control
- The amulet cannot be safely destroyed without purification first

---

## ABOUT VELOUR CITY

- Velour City has an unusual concentration of supernatural activity
- The city functions as an unintentional spiritual amplifier
- The Harbour District has the highest density of residual spiritual impressions
- Portland Wharf operates as an informal transit point for supernatural elements entering the city
- The Shelf contains three locations where the boundary between the physical world and the adjacent layer is measurably thin
- Inspector Crimson has an established reputation in Velour City's supernatural circles
- Ordinary residents cannot perceive spirits unless a spirit chooses to manifest or becomes dangerously agitated

---

## ABOUT INSPECTOR CRIMSON

- Inspector Crimson is Iris Wilson
- Inspector Crimson's abilities are perception and purification only — no physical combat, no object manipulation
- Inspector Crimson has never publicly failed a case
- Inspector Crimson has never encountered a Class Three anchor before this case
- The disguise — crimson hair and monocle — is not sufficient to conceal Iris's identity from anyone paying close attention
- Iris knows the disguise is insufficient and has chosen not to resolve this

## RELATIONSHIP FACTS

- Iris and Takumi have a provisional undefined working arrangement — no formal contract exists
- Iris has made three small adjustments to the office for Takumi's benefit without acknowledging them
- Takumi has begun leaving personal items at the agency without noticing he is doing this
- Neither has discussed the nature of their arrangement directly
- The harbormaster has spoken to Iris exactly three times — always initiated by the harbormaster
- The night guard at Portland Wharf has an undisclosed arrangement with Iris

- Iris transforming casually or without genuine necessity
- The Beryl Tenant being destroyed rather than purified
- Any character knowing Iris and Inspector Crimson are the same person at story start
- The identity or motivation of whoever brought the amulet to Velour City — this is sealed until the relevant plot event
