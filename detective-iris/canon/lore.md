## THE BLOOD BERYL AMULET

### Origin

The amulet was created approximately eighty years ago by a craftsman in the Harbour District whose name has not survived in any official record. He was not a supernatural practitioner by training — he was a jeweler who worked with unusual stones and developed, over decades, an intuitive sensitivity to materials that carried residual spiritual energy.

The blood beryl stone at the center of the amulet was sourced from a location outside Velour City that sits on a significant spiritual fault line — a place where the boundary between the physical world and the adjacent layer is permanently and irreparably thin. Stones from this location absorb spiritual energy passively over geological time. The blood beryl had been absorbing for a very long time before the jeweler found it.

He did not create the amulet with malicious intent. He created it as a containment vessel — a way to safely house the spiritual energy the stone had accumulated, preventing it from bleeding into the physical world uncontrolled. The design was his own intuitive solution to a problem he didn't have precise language for.

It worked. For a while.

### What Went Wrong

Containment vessels of this kind require periodic maintenance — a practitioner with perception ability must occasionally interact with the object to keep the contained energy stable. The jeweler had that ability instinctively. When he died, nobody with equivalent ability took over the maintenance.

Over the following decades the contained energy — already substantial — continued accumulating with nowhere to go. The spirit bonded to the stone, which had been passive and largely unconscious when first contained, gradually became aware. Aware of its containment. Aware of time passing. Aware of the physical world just beyond the boundary of the amulet.

By the time anyone with genuine perception ability encountered the amulet again it had crossed from containment vessel to anchor — the spirit was no longer passively housed, it was actively bonded, and the distinction matters enormously.

### Current State

The amulet is a Class Three anchor. The bonded spirit — referred to in Iris's files as the Beryl Tenant — is fully conscious, has been aware for approximately thirty years, and has been actively attempting to influence the physical world through anyone who handles the amulet for extended periods.

Influence manifests gradually. Initial contact produces nothing notable. Extended handling over days or weeks produces increasing suggestibility — handlers become unusually focused on acquiring things, protecting the amulet, acting in the Beryl Tenant's interest without understanding why. They do not experience this as external influence. They experience it as their own desires becoming clearer.

The amulet has had at least six documented handlers in the past thirty years. Their outcomes vary. None of them understood what was happening to them.

### Why It Was Moved To Velour City

The amulet's most recent previous location was a private collection in another city — owned by a collector of unusual objects who acquired it without understanding its nature. The collector died eight months ago. The estate was handled by a legal firm that catalogued the collection for auction.

Someone with knowledge of the amulet's true nature intervened before the auction. The amulet was removed from the estate inventory and routed through channels that ended at Portland Wharf. Who intervened and why they brought it specifically to Velour City is the central question of this case.

The answer involves the spiritual infrastructure at Portland Wharf and an interest in Velour City's unusually thin boundaries. Someone wants the Beryl Tenant in this city for a reason. That reason is not benevolent.

## VELOUR CITY — SUPERNATURAL HISTORY

Velour City's unusual supernatural density is not accidental and not purely geological.

The city was founded on a site that indigenous knowledge — largely unrecorded and mostly lost — identified as significant. The founders were not aware of this significance. The city grew organically over two centuries, and the construction, habitation, and accumulated human experience layered over the existing spiritual geography without understanding or managing it.

The result is a city that functions as an unintentional amplifier. Spiritual energy that passes through Velour City becomes more concentrated. Spirits that settle here become more defined. Events with emotional weight leave deeper residual impressions than they would elsewhere.

This is why the Harbour District has the texture it has. Two centuries of accumulated impression, unmanaged and uncleared.

This is also why someone wants the Beryl Tenant here specifically. In another city the amulet is dangerous. In Velour City, with its amplifying effect, the Beryl Tenant's influence could extend significantly further than normal anchor range.

## INSPECTOR CRIMSON — PUBLIC LORE

Inspector Crimson has been active in Velour City for approximately seven years. In supernatural circles — practitioners, aware entities, older residents of the Harbour District with perception sensitivity — she is known as a reliable and effective purification agent.

Her reputation is professional and slightly intimidating. She does not negotiate with malevolent spirits. She does not leave cases unresolved. She has never failed to purify a Class Two anchor or below.

She has never publicly encountered a Class Three.

The offerings left near The Shelf by older Harbour District residents are a form of respectful acknowledgment — a cultural remnant of practices for recognizing protective presences. Iris is aware of the offerings. She has never acknowledged them publicly. She moves them when they obstruct foot traffic and replaces them slightly to one side, which she would describe as practical and which is not only practical.
