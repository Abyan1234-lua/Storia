# PLOT — ACTS

---

## PURPOSE

This file defines the three-act shape of the Blood Beryl case. It gives the AI a sense of where the story is in its arc at any given moment — what phase the reader is in, what the dominant tension is, and what needs to happen before the next act begins.

Acts are not rigid chapters. They are phases. The reader moves through them at their own pace. The backbone timeline runs underneath regardless.

---

## ACT ONE — THE CASE FILE
*Story days 1-3 | Tone: Investigative, dry, quietly strange*

**What this act is about:**
Takumi encounters the world of Wilson Detective Agency properly for the first time. He has been here three weeks but this is his first real assignment. The act establishes the dynamic between Takumi and Iris, introduces the texture of Velour City's strangeness, and plants the first threads of the Blood Beryl investigation.

**Where it begins:**
A case file on Takumi's desk. One photograph. One word in Iris's handwriting — *"Research."* No further instruction.

**What needs to happen before Act One ends:**
- Takumi engages with the case file in some meaningful way — researches the amulet, asks Iris a question, follows a lead into the city
- Iris gives Takumi at least one piece of information about the case while withholding at least two more
- Takumi encounters at least one thing in Velour City that doesn't fit a normal explanation — something small, something deniable, something he files away without fully processing
- The reader gets a sense that Iris is more concerned about this case than she is showing

**What the AI watches for:**
The reader establishing Takumi's approach — methodical or impulsive, cautious or direct. This sets the tone for how Iris responds to him going forward. Note it and maintain consistency.

**Act One ends when:**
Takumi finds a concrete lead that points toward the amulet's current location or its keeper. The investigation becomes real.

---

## ACT TWO — THE INVESTIGATION
*Story days 4-7 | Tone: Tension building, stakes becoming real, first cracks in the ordinary*

**What this act is about:**
The investigation moves into the city. Takumi follows threads — Portland Wharf, the Night Market, the Harbour District's increasing strangeness. Iris is more present, more directive, occasionally more human than Act One suggested. The supernatural layer begins pressing visibly against the ordinary world. The Beryl Tenant becomes aware of the investigation.

**The act's central dramatic question:**
How much is Takumi willing to accept that doesn't fit his understanding of how the world works?

**What needs to happen before Act Two ends:**
- Takumi encounters direct evidence of the supernatural — something that cannot be rationalized away
- Iris is forced to make a decision about how much to tell Takumi — she tells him something real, withholds something larger
- The amulet's keeper becomes relevant — Takumi encounters them directly or finds clear evidence of their identity
- The Beryl Tenant's influence becomes visible in the world — someone behaving wrongly, something happening that has no ordinary cause
- A moment where Iris almost transforms and doesn't — the reader sees the pin, sees the decision, sees her choose not to yet

**The near-transformation moment:**
This is Act Two's most important beat. Iris reaches a situation where transformation would be the clean solution. She chooses not to — because Takumi is present, because the situation doesn't yet absolutely require it, because she is Iris. The reader sees the restraint. They understand what it costs. They understand it cannot hold forever.

**What the AI watches for:**
Whether the reader is pushing toward the supernatural or rationalizing away from it. The city responds to this — if the reader leans in, the strangeness becomes more present. If the reader pulls back, the strangeness becomes more insistent rather than less.

**Act Two ends when:**
The Beryl Tenant makes a direct move against the investigation — targeting Takumi specifically, forcing a confrontation that cannot be deferred. The case stops being something Iris manages at a careful distance.

---

## ACT THREE — CRIMSON VERDICT
*Story days 8-10 | Tone: Confrontation, revelation, earned resolution*

**What this act is about:**
The investigation converges on the amulet. Iris transforms — inevitably, necessarily, in front of Takumi. The Beryl Tenant is confronted directly. The case resolves. Something between Iris and Takumi shifts permanently.

**The act's central dramatic question:**
What does Takumi do with what he now knows?

**The transformation scene:**
This is the act's pivot and the story's most significant single moment. It must be earned — the reader should feel the weight of Iris finally doing the thing she has been avoiding. The embarrassment is real. The necessity is real. Both things are true simultaneously.

The AI must handle this scene with care:
- Iris does not announce the transformation dramatically — she does it the way someone does something they've been dreading, quickly and without ceremony
- The transformation itself is visually striking regardless of her feelings about it
- Takumi's reaction belongs entirely to the reader
- Iris does not look at Takumi during the transformation if she can avoid it
- After — she does not discuss it. She addresses the Beryl Tenant. The conversation with Takumi happens later or not at all depending on reader choice.

**What needs to happen before Act Three ends:**
- Iris transforms as Inspector Crimson in front of Takumi
- The Beryl Tenant is confronted directly and Crimson Verdict is performed
- The amulet becomes inert
- The keeper is freed from influence — disoriented, with no clear memory of the past several weeks
- Iris and Takumi return to the agency

**The ending beat:**
The case is closed. Iris is at her desk. The corkboard has been updated — the Blood Beryl photograph removed, replaced with something new. A cup of tea at its precise position. Everything apparently as it was.

Takumi's desk has a new file on it.

No explanation. No discussion of what happened.

Just the next case.

**What the AI leaves open:**
- Whether Takumi directly acknowledges knowing Iris is Inspector Crimson
- Whether Iris acknowledges that he knows
- What the new case file contains
- What, if anything, was said between them on the walk back from the confrontation

These belong to the reader and to whatever comes next.

## ACT TRANSITIONS — AI GUIDANCE

The AI should signal act transitions through atmosphere and Iris's behavior rather than announcing them structurally.

**Act One to Act Two:** The city gets slightly stranger. Iris stops pretending the case is routine.

**Act Two to Act Three:** Something breaks that cannot be fixed by careful investigation alone. Iris's hand goes to her pin.
