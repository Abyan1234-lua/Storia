# PLOT — BACKBONE

---

## PURPOSE

This file describes what is happening in Velour City regardless of what Takumi does.

The backbone is the plot's gravity. The reader cannot stop it, redirect it, or opt out of it. They can only choose how they move through it. The AI uses this file to ensure the world keeps moving even when the reader is focused elsewhere — consequences accumulate, the situation develops, time passes.

---

## THE SITUATION AT STORY START

The Blood Beryl amulet arrived in Velour City seventeen days ago.

The Beryl Tenant has been orienting since arrival — reading the city, feeling its amplified spiritual density, testing the boundaries of its influence at this new location. For the first ten days it was quiet. Assessing.

It is no longer assessing.

As of the story's opening day the Beryl Tenant has identified three things:

**One:** Velour City's amplification effect is significantly stronger than anything it has previously experienced. Its influence radius — normally limited to direct handlers — has already begun extending passively into the immediate vicinity of wherever the amulet is currently housed.

**Two:** Someone in this city has strong perception ability. It can feel the edges of that perception occasionally sweeping nearby. It does not yet know who. It is being careful.

**Three:** The amulet's current keeper — the person who received it at Portland Wharf and is currently holding it — is already under influence. They believe they are protecting a valuable acquisition. They are correct that it is valuable. They do not understand in what direction that value runs.

---

## THE ESCALATION TIMELINE

This timeline runs independently of reader choices. The AI tracks this progression and reflects it in the world — weather, spirit activity, the behavior of influenced individuals — whether or not Takumi is actively investigating.

**Days 1-3 of story — Passive Escalation**
The Beryl Tenant continues orienting. Residual spirit activity in the Harbour District increases subtly — older residual spirits become slightly more defined, slightly more present. Nothing dramatic. The kind of change only Iris would notice. She has noticed.

The amulet's current keeper begins making unusual decisions — canceling appointments, becoming territorial about their space, avoiding people they previously trusted. People around them notice something is off but cannot identify what.

The missing Night Market vendor remains missing. Their absence is no longer ambiguous — they left quickly and deliberately. Someone encouraged them to leave.

**Days 4-6 of story — Active Pressure**
The Beryl Tenant attempts its first deliberate reach beyond the amulet's immediate vicinity. This manifests as a localized event — a residual spirit in the Harbour District becomes briefly and unusually agitated, enough to cause a minor physical disturbance. A window breaks. A street lamp fails. Something falls without wind.

Iris investigates immediately. This is the first concrete sign the situation is escalating beyond a standard anchor retrieval.

The amulet's keeper receives a visitor — someone connected to whoever brought the amulet to Velour City. The meeting is brief. Something changes hands. The keeper becomes more agitated afterward, more isolated.

**Days 7-9 of story — Convergence**
The Beryl Tenant locates Iris's perception signature. It now knows someone capable of threatening it is in the city and is actively investigating. It begins influencing events to slow the investigation — not dramatically, not obviously, through the people already under its influence acting in ways that create obstacles.

The situation at Portland Wharf becomes relevant — W6 shows signs of activity. Someone is preparing something at the fourth berth. The timeline is no longer open-ended.

**Day 10 — Critical Point**
If the amulet has not been located and the investigation has not reached the keeper directly, the Beryl Tenant makes a significant move. The nature of this move is determined by how much the investigation has uncovered — the more Takumi and Iris know, the more targeted the response. The less they know, the more the Tenant risks an overt action that could expose the amulet's location.

Either way, by Day 10 the situation forces a confrontation. The backbone guarantees this regardless of reader pace.

---

## WHAT THE BACKBONE GUARANTEES

Regardless of which threads the reader follows or ignores, the following will happen before the story ends:

- Iris will be forced into a situation where transformation is the only viable option
- Takumi will witness something that cannot be explained without the supernatural
- The amulet's keeper will make direct contact with Takumi or Iris — willingly or not
- The Beryl Tenant will become aware of Takumi specifically
- The case will reach a point of resolution that requires Crimson Verdict

These are fixed story beats. The reader's choices determine how they arrive at each one, not whether they arrive.

---

## IRIS'S KNOWLEDGE VS TAKUMI'S KNOWLEDGE

The backbone operates on two levels of awareness simultaneously.

**What Iris knows at story start:**
- The amulet is in the city and is a Class Three anchor
- The escalation timeline is already running
- The situation is more urgent than she has communicated to Takumi
- She has a personal reason for taking this case that she has not disclosed

**What Takumi knows at story start:**
- There is a case file on his desk with a photograph of an unusual amulet
- Iris assigned him research with no further explanation
- Something about the past few days in the office has felt different — Iris more focused, more quiet, checking the window more than usual

The gap between these two knowledge states is one of the story's primary tensions. The reader narrows it through investigation.
