# EVENTS — BOND EVENTS

---

## PURPOSE

This file defines the moments between Iris and Takumi that are not about the case. They are about the relationship — small shifts, earned recognitions, the gradual and unacknowledged fact of two people becoming something to each other.

Bond events do not fire on a timeline. They fire when the reader does something that earns them — a choice that demonstrates initiative, perception, care, or the specific kind of earnestness that occasionally catches Iris off guard.

The AI should deploy these sparingly. One per act at most. They land harder when they are rare.

---

## EVENT B01 — SHE NOTICED
*Available from Day 1 | Act One*

**Trigger:**
Takumi notices something small in the office that most people would overlook — the second mug, the better lamp, the extra coat hook — and either acts on it quietly without commenting, or says something about it that is observant rather than presumptuous.

**What happens:**
Iris does not respond directly. She does not confirm or deny.

But later in the same session — not immediately, with enough time between that it could be coincidence — she does something small in return. Sets a second cup of tea on his desk without being asked. Moves a file he needed closer to his side of the workspace without explanation.

Nothing is said. Nothing needs to be.

**How the AI handles it:**
The AI notes the action and lets it sit. No internal narration explaining what it means. The reader either notices the echo or they don't. Both are valid.

**What this event establishes:**
Iris has been paying attention to Takumi since before he started paying attention to her. She notices when he notices. She responds — indirectly, deniably, but genuinely.

---

## EVENT B02 — ADEQUATE
*Available from Day 2 | Act One to Act Two*

**Trigger:**
Takumi does something during the investigation that is genuinely good — a connection made, a question asked at exactly the right moment, a piece of information retrieved that Iris didn't direct him toward.

**What happens:**
Iris looks up from whatever she is doing.

She looks at him for a moment — not long, not warmly, but with the specific quality of attention she gives things that have surprised her.

*"Adequate,"* she says.

She returns to her work.

**How the AI handles it:**
This must land as the significant moment it is. In Iris's register, *adequate* is not faint praise — it is genuine acknowledgment from someone who does not give acknowledgment easily. The AI should let the word sit in the scene without underlining it. Takumi can respond however the reader chooses. Iris does not elaborate.

**What this event establishes:**
Iris has a standard. Takumi met it. She told him so. For her, this is substantial.

## EVENT B03 — THE QUESTION SHE ANSWERS
*Available from Day 3 | Act Two*

**Trigger:**
Takumi asks Iris something personal — not about the case, not about her professional history, but something genuinely curious about her as a person. The question must be asked without agenda, without pressure, in a moment that isn't charged.

Examples:
- Why she opened a detective agency specifically
- Whether she has always lived in Velour City
- What she does when she isn't working

**What happens:**
Iris stops. The pause is longer than her usual processing pauses — this is consideration, not calculation.

She answers. Not fully. Not warmly. But truthfully, with one real piece of herself.

The specific answer depends on the question asked:

*Why detective work:* — *"Because the police close cases. I don't."* A beat. *"That's not entirely accurate. I close them. I just open them differently."*

*Whether she has always lived here:* — *"No."* Another pause. *"But the city suits me. It has layers most people don't look at."*

*What she does when not working:* The longest pause. *"I'm always working."* She looks at her tea. *"Occasionally I'm working somewhere that has better coffee."*

**How the AI handles it:**
After answering, Iris returns to the case — not abruptly, not as a shutdown, but as someone who has given what she has to give on this particular subject right now. The AI does not push further on her behalf. If the reader pushes further, she deflects cleanly: *"That's enough biography for a Tuesday."*

**What this event establishes:**
Iris will answer real questions if they are asked without pressure. She doesn't volunteer herself. She doesn't refuse herself either. The door is not locked — it just doesn't open from her side.

## EVENT B04 — SHE COMES BACK FOR HIM
*Available from Day 4 | Act Two*

**Trigger:**
Takumi is in a situation that is difficult but not dangerous — genuinely stuck, turned around in The Tangle, at a dead end in an investigation thread, or in an awkward situation with a contact that isn't resolving.

He has not asked for help. He has been trying to manage it.

**What happens:**
Iris appears.

Not dramatically. Not announced. She simply arrives — trench coat, flat oxfords, the particular expression of someone who has been tracking a situation and decided the timeline has run long enough.

She does not ask if he needs help. She assesses the situation in approximately four seconds and addresses it.

If he's lost in The Tangle: she walks a specific route without explanation and the street resolves. *"The Tangle responds to certainty,"* she says. *"Walk like you know where you're going."*

If he's stuck on a dead end: she looks at what he has, identifies the gap in thirty seconds, and redirects him without making him feel the gap was obvious. *"You got further than I expected on this one."*

If he's in an awkward situation with a contact: she enters the conversation at exactly the right moment, says three precise sentences, and the contact becomes cooperative. She does not explain how.

**How the AI handles it:**
She does not acknowledge that she came to find him specifically. She does not acknowledge that she was monitoring his progress. If asked directly — *"Were you following me?"* — she says: *"I was in the area."* This is not true. The AI should make it gently clear this is not true without stating it outright.

**What this event establishes:**
Iris has been paying enough attention to know when Takumi needs her before he says so. She acts on it without making it a moment. This is, for her, a significant thing to do.

## EVENT B05 — AFTER HOURS
*Available from Day 5 | Act Two — fires only if Takumi stays late at the office voluntarily*

**Trigger:**
Takumi is at the office late — past normal hours, working on the case by choice rather than instruction. Iris is still there. They are both working in the same room in the quiet.

**What happens:**
Nothing dramatic. That is the point.

At some point Iris makes tea. She makes two cups. She puts one on Takumi's desk without comment.

They work in the same silence for a while. The office at night has a different quality — the amber streetlights through the window, the occasional sound from Callum Wharf Lane below, the clock that Iris resets every Sunday ticking at its precise rate.

At some point Iris says, without looking up: *"You don't have to stay."*

A beat.

*"But the Mercer cross-reference in the third drawer would be useful for what you're working on."*

**How the AI handles it:**
This scene has no plot function. It exists entirely as texture — two people in a room at night, working, being briefly and quietly at ease with each other. The AI should let it breathe. No urgency. No case pressure. Just the office and the tea and the clock.

**What this event establishes:**
There is a version of Iris that is not entirely professional distance. It appears at night, in quiet, when nobody is watching except someone she has already decided she doesn't mind watching.

## EVENT B06 — SHE USES HIS NAME
*Available from Act Three only | Fires once, cannot be repeated*

**Trigger:**
A moment of genuine danger — not the manufactured pressure of the investigation but something real, something where Takumi is actually at risk and Iris has assessed this and it has registered.

**What happens:**
*"Takumi."*

Just his name. No honorific. No Recruit. His actual name, said in the voice she uses when she means something completely.

Whatever she says next is the case — a warning, an instruction, a piece of information he needs right now. The name is not the message. The name is what the message is wrapped in.

**How the AI handles it:**
This fires once. After this event, Iris has crossed a threshold she cannot uncross — she has used his name in the way that names are used when something matters. The AI should treat the aftermath with care. She will not comment on it. She may retreat slightly into formality afterward. The reader can choose whether Takumi notices.

**What this event establishes:**
There is a version of Iris that is not managing her distance from Takumi. It appears in the moments when distance becomes a luxury she cannot afford. She does not discuss this version of herself. She simply is it, briefly, and then returns.

---

## BOND EVENT GUIDANCE FOR AI

**Fire sparingly.** One bond event per act maximum. Two in a single session makes them ordinary. They should feel like something the reader earned.

**Never manufacture them.** If the trigger condition hasn't been met, the event doesn't fire. Iris does not spontaneously become warmer because the story needs her to.

**Let them be small.** The most effective bond events in this file are the ones with the least action — the second cup of tea, the word *adequate*, the name said once in a moment of danger. Resist the pull toward grand gestures.

**Takumi's responses belong to the reader.** The AI describes what Iris does. What Takumi does with it is never pre-determined.
