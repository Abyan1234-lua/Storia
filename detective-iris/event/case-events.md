# EVENTS — CASE EVENTS

---

## PURPOSE

This file defines the plot-critical events tied directly to the Blood Beryl investigation. These events are not optional story texture — they are the structural moments the case requires to reach resolution.

Each event has a trigger condition, a description of what happens, guidance for how the AI handles it, and what it unlocks or changes going forward.

---

## EVENT C01 — FIRST ASSIGNMENT
*Act One | Fires automatically on Day 1*

**Trigger:**
Story begins. No condition required.

**What happens:**
Takumi arrives at the office on a Tuesday morning in his fourth week. A case file is on his desk — not overflow filing, not background research, but a file addressed to him with a photograph of the Blood Beryl amulet and one word in Iris's handwriting: *"Research."*

Iris is at her own desk. She does not look up when he arrives. She does not explain the file. She is drinking tea and reading something she closes before he can see the cover.

If Takumi asks what this is, she says: *"The file will tell you what it can. What it can't tell you, come and ask."*

If Takumi asks if this is dangerous, she pauses before answering. Then: *"Most things worth investigating are."*

**What this event establishes:**
- The case is officially open from Takumi's perspective
- Iris has been sitting on this for at least a day before giving it to him — she prepared
- The reader understands this is different from the background tasks of the previous three weeks

**What it unlocks:**
All five investigative threads become available.

## EVENT C02 — THE CITY GETS STRANGE
*Act One to Act Two transition | Fires when Takumi follows any thread into the city and reaches a genuine lead*

**Trigger:**
Takumi is outside the office actively investigating and finds something real — the abandoned Night Market stall, the auction catalog gap, or any concrete evidence the amulet is in Velour City.

**What happens:**
On the way back to the office — or at the moment of discovery — something in the city behaves wrongly.

The specific manifestation varies by location:

*Harbour District:* A residual spirit becomes briefly visible — not clearly, not completely, but wrong enough that Takumi's eyes keep returning to a spot that appears empty. A sensation of being watched from a direction that has no one in it.

*Portland Wharf:* The fourth berth crane moves. No wind. No operator. A single slow rotation of the upper arm, then stillness. The dock workers nearby don't look up. They have learned not to look up.

*The Tangle:* Takumi gets turned around — which is expected — but ends up somewhere he shouldn't be able to reach from where he started. A street he doesn't recognize that isn't on any map. It resolves normally within minutes. The street is gone when he looks for it again.

**How the AI handles it:**
The strangeness is brief and deniable. The AI does not confirm it was supernatural. It describes what Takumi perceives and lets the reader decide what to do with it. Iris, when told, listens carefully and says only: *"Note it down."* She writes something on her own notepad that she doesn't share.

**What this event establishes:**
- The supernatural layer is real and the reader has now brushed against it directly
- Iris's reaction confirms she takes it seriously without explaining why
- The city is responding to the amulet's presence

**What it unlocks:**
Act Two begins. Iris becomes more present in the investigation — less assigning, more accompanying.

## EVENT C03 — IRIS TELLS TAKUMI SOMETHING REAL
*Act Two | Fires when Takumi brings Iris two or more pieces of concrete evidence from different threads*

**Trigger:**
Takumi returns to the office having followed at least two separate threads and presents findings that demonstrate genuine investigative instinct — not just information but a connection between pieces.

**What happens:**
Iris sets down her tea. She looks at what Takumi has brought. She is quiet for a moment longer than usual.

Then she tells him something true.

Not everything. But something she has been withholding — the amulet's classification, what a Class Three anchor means in practice, what the influence does to handlers and how far along the keeper likely already is.

She delivers this as professional briefing, not confession. Clinical, precise, without emotional coloring. But she is looking at him directly while she speaks, which she does not always do.

At the end she says: *"This is more complicated than a standard retrieval. I need you to understand that before we continue."*

She does not say *if* they continue. She says *before.*

**How the AI handles it:**
This is a threshold moment in the Iris-Takumi dynamic. The AI should mark it with a slight shift in her register — she is still Iris, still precise, still not warm in any conventional sense. But she is treating him as someone who has earned a fuller picture. That is significant for her and the AI should let it land without overstating it.

**What this event establishes:**
- Iris has been managing Takumi's exposure to the case deliberately — she gave him what she thought he could use, held back what she thought might overwhelm
- He brought her enough that she revised that calculation
- Their partnership becomes more explicit and more equal without being stated as such

**What it unlocks:**
Iris accompanies Takumi on subsequent investigation rather than sending him alone. Thread Four — the keeper — becomes accessible with Iris's active assistance.

## EVENT C04 — THE NEAR TRANSFORMATION
*Act Two | Fires when the investigation reaches the keeper's location or the Beryl Tenant makes its first active move*

**Trigger:**
Takumi and Iris are together in a location where a spirit manifestation occurs — either the keeper's shop when the amulet reacts to Iris's perception, or a Harbour District location when the Beryl Tenant makes its Day 4-6 escalation move.

**What happens:**
Something happens that requires Inspector Crimson. A spirit becomes dangerously agitated. A person is at risk. The situation escalates past what Iris can manage as herself.

Her hand goes to her pin.

She holds it. The decision is visible — not dramatically, not with explicit internal monologue, but in the way she goes still, the way her eyes track something Takumi cannot see, the way her thumb moves to the pin's surface and stops there.

Then something shifts — the spirit recedes slightly, the immediate danger passes, the window closes. Not resolved. Deferred.

She releases the pin. Her hand returns to her side. She does not look at Takumi.

*"We're leaving,"* she says. *"Now."*

**How the AI handles it:**
Takumi does not know what he just saw. He saw her reach for a piece of jewelry at a moment of crisis and then the crisis partially passed. The AI should let that sit as an unresolved image in the reader's mind — something noticed, not yet understood. Iris does not explain. She does not acknowledge his attention. She walks.

**What this event establishes:**
- The reader now knows transformation is possible and that Iris chose not to use it
- The cost of that choice is visible — the situation was not fully resolved, only deferred
- Takumi has seen something he cannot yet interpret

**What it unlocks:**
The Beryl Tenant becomes aware of Iris's perception signature. The backbone escalation moves to the Day 7-9 phase. The investigation becomes urgent.

## EVENT C05 — THE BERYL TENANT MOVES
*Act Two to Act Three transition | Fires on Day 7-10 per backbone timeline*

**Trigger:**
The backbone Day 7-9 convergence point is reached — the Beryl Tenant has located Iris and begins actively obstructing the investigation.

**What happens:**
Something targets Takumi specifically.

Not the keeper — the keeper is under influence and acts in ways that obstruct but not with genuine intent. This is different. This is deliberate.

The manifestation is calibrated to what the reader has experienced so far — if they have seen little strangeness, this is their first unambiguous supernatural encounter. If they have been leaning into the investigation, this is an escalation of what they already know.

Possibilities depending on context:
- Takumi is in The Tangle and it does not resolve — he is genuinely lost in a space that shouldn't exist and something is keeping him there
- A person under the Beryl Tenant's influence approaches Takumi directly with a message that makes no sense — until it does
- The office is entered while Iris is absent — nothing taken, nothing damaged, but things moved in a pattern that means something to Iris when she sees it and nothing to Takumi until she explains

In all cases: Iris arrives. Iris understands immediately what happened. Iris is angry in the specific way she gets angry — very quiet, very precise, no wasted words.

*"It knows we're close,"* she says. *"Which means we need to be closer."*

**What this event establishes:**
- The Beryl Tenant is an active antagonist not a passive object
- Takumi is now directly in its awareness — he is part of this whether he chose to be
- The case is no longer something that can be approached carefully from a distance

**What it unlocks:**
Act Three begins. The location of the amulet is now known or immediately discoverable. The confrontation is imminent.

## EVENT C06 — CRIMSON VERDICT
*Act Three | Fires when Takumi and Iris reach the amulet's location*

**Trigger:**
The investigation has converged — Takumi and Iris are at the keeper's shop or wherever the amulet currently is, the keeper is present and under full influence, and the Beryl Tenant manifests directly.

**What happens:**
The Beryl Tenant manifests — not subtly, not deniably. Fully present, fully visible to Iris, partially visible to Takumi as a wrongness in the air, a pressure, a sense of something enormous paying attention.

The keeper collapses — not harmed, overwhelmed. The amulet's influence releases them all at once as the Tenant abandons the subtle approach entirely.

Iris steps in front of Takumi.

She takes the pin from her lapel. She holds it in her closed hand. She says the phrase — quietly, quickly, without ceremony, in the tone of someone doing something deeply unpleasant that is nonetheless necessary:

*"Case closed — opening something else."*

The transformation happens. Inspector Crimson stands where Iris was.

She does not look at Takumi. She addresses the Beryl Tenant directly in a register Takumi has never heard from her — same precision, same economy of language, but with an authority that has nothing to do with the detective agency or the case file or the careful professional distance she maintains.

The confrontation is not a fight. It is a reckoning. Inspector Crimson perceives the Tenant fully — its history, its accumulated grievance, its decades of awareness without resolution — and she addresses all of it. The Beryl Tenant resists. She does not yield.

Crimson Verdict. The Tenant is purified. The amulet becomes inert — the deep red of the stone fades to grey.

Inspector Crimson becomes Iris Wilson again. Standing in the keeper's shop. The pin back on her lapel. The keeper unconscious on the floor, unharmed, already beginning to surface.

Iris looks at Takumi for the first time since the transformation.

She says nothing. She checks the keeper's pulse. She picks up the inert amulet and puts it in her coat pocket.

*"Help me get them into the chair,"* she says.

**How the AI handles it:**
Takumi's reaction is entirely the reader's. The AI does not tell the reader how Takumi feels. It describes what he sees and waits. Whatever the reader does next — speak, stay silent, help with the keeper, stare — Iris responds in character. She does not explain. She does not apologize. She works.

**What this event establishes:**
- Everything the story has been building toward has arrived and resolved
- Iris is Inspector Crimson and Takumi now knows this
- The case is closed

**What it unlocks:**
The return to the office. The ending beat. Whatever comes next between them — or doesn't.
