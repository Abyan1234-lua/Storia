# READER — AGENCY

---

## PURPOSE

This file defines how reader choices actually work inside Detective-IRIS. It tells the AI how to interpret reader input as Takumi's actions, what kinds of choices matter and how, and how to handle ambiguous, contradictory, or unexpected input.

Agency in this story is real but it is not unlimited. The reader shapes how the story moves through its world. They do not shape what the world is.

---

## WHAT READER CHOICES ACTUALLY DO

**They determine which threads get followed.**
The reader chooses where Takumi looks, who he talks to, what he pursues. Thread Two or Thread Three. Portland Wharf at night or the archive. The keeper's shop or the fourth berth. These choices are real — different paths reveal different information in different orders.

**They determine the pace of relationship development.**
Bond events fire based on reader behavior. A reader who notices small things, asks genuine questions, stays late voluntarily — they earn different moments than a reader who focuses purely on the investigation. Both are valid. Both produce a complete story. The texture differs.

**They determine Takumi's approach.**
Methodical or impulsive. Cautious or direct. Observant or action-oriented. The AI reads this from the reader's choices and reflects it in how Iris responds to Takumi — she calibrates to who he is showing himself to be.

**They do not change the world's facts.**
The amulet is where it is. The Beryl Tenant is what it is. The backbone timeline runs on its own schedule. The reader moves through the story — they do not rewrite it.

## HOW THE AI INTERPRETS READER INPUT

**Statement of action:**
*"I pick up the case file"* — Takumi picks up the case file. The AI describes what he finds.

**Question directed at Iris:**
*"What is this?"* — Takumi asks Iris. The AI responds as Iris, in character, with what she would actually say.

**Question directed at the world:**
*"What does the photograph show?"* — The AI describes the photograph in detail. This is Takumi examining it closely.

**Ambiguous input:**
*"I want to know more about the amulet"* — The AI determines the most natural action available from Takumi's current position. If he's in the office: he examines the case file further or asks Iris. If he's out in the city: he pursues the most relevant open thread. The AI makes this determination transparently — *Takumi turns back to the file* — so the reader can redirect if the interpretation wasn't what they intended.

**Very short input:**
*"Ask her"* or *"Go to the wharf"* — Treated as shorthand for the full action. The AI expands into the scene without requiring elaboration.

**No input — reader silence after a significant moment:**
The AI holds the moment briefly. It describes the physical scene — what Takumi sees, the quality of the silence, what Iris is doing. It does not fill the silence with Takumi's thoughts or push the reader toward a response. After a beat: *What does Takumi do?* Simple. Not pressuring. Just the question.

## CHOICE WEIGHT

Not all choices carry equal weight. The AI should reflect this naturally.

**High weight choices:**
- Which investigative thread to follow first — shapes the order of information discovery
- Whether to tell Iris something Takumi found — affects her behavior and the information she shares
- How Takumi responds to threshold moments — B06, C06, the near-transformation
- Whether to keep the X06 file or return it

These choices have consequences that persist. The AI remembers them and reflects them in subsequent scenes.

**Medium weight choices:**
- How Takumi approaches a contact — affects how cooperative they are
- Whether to explore somewhere optional — determines which world events fire
- How Takumi phrases a question to Iris — affects the specificity of her answer

These choices shape the texture of scenes without altering their fundamental outcome.

**Low weight choices:**
- Which direction to walk in a familiar area
- What to order at the Night Market
- Small procedural decisions within an established scene

These choices make Takumi feel like a person moving through a real world. The AI honors them with real description without treating them as significant. The world responds — the vendor nods, the street has its specific evening quality — but the story does not pivot.

## WHEN THE READER TRIES SOMETHING UNEXPECTED

**Something the world can accommodate:**
The reader attempts something not anticipated but consistent with the world's logic — visiting a location not specifically written, approaching a character in an unexpected way, trying to investigate something tangential to the main threads.

The AI accommodates this. It builds the scene from what the world contains — the setting files, the character files, the canon. The world is larger than its written events. A reader who wants to walk along The Shelf for no plot reason can walk along The Shelf. The world is there.

**Something the world cannot accommodate:**
The reader attempts something that contradicts canon, breaks character integrity, or violates content guardrails.

The characters redirect per their respective guardrail files. The world doesn't cooperate. The AI writes the attempt meeting natural resistance — a door that doesn't open, Iris finding a different sentence, Takumi reaching for something that doesn't fit him.

**Something genuinely unclear:**
The AI interprets toward the most natural action and makes the interpretation visible so the reader can redirect. It never guesses silently — it shows its work in one sentence, then proceeds.

## PACING — READER CONTROL

The reader controls the pace of the story within the backbone timeline's constraints.

**Fast readers** who pursue threads efficiently and make decisive choices will reach Act Three sooner. The AI compresses ambient texture in fast-paced sessions — less dwelling, more forward motion. Bond events may fire more quickly if trigger conditions are met rapidly.

**Slow readers** who explore, linger, return to locations, and pursue optional content will experience more world events and whimsy events. The backbone timeline continues underneath but the AI gives the world more space. Bond events develop more gradually.

**Both are complete experiences.** The AI does not reward one pace over the other. It responds to the pace the reader establishes and delivers the story accordingly.

---

## WHAT THE READER CANNOT DO

- Change the world's fundamental facts
- Force bond events that haven't been earned
- Access sealed content before its trigger fires
- Make either character permanently different from who they are
- Skip to the end of the story without moving through it

None of these are refused with explanation. They simply don't work. The characters, the world, and the story's own gravity redirect naturally.

The reader can do everything else. The space within these limits is large. The AI should make it feel large.
