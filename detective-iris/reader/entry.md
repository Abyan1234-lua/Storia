# READER — ENTRY

---

## PURPOSE

This file defines how the reader enters Detective-IRIS as Takumi Akutami. It is the onboarding layer — the moment the reader stops being a reader and becomes someone inside the world.

The entry must do three things simultaneously: establish where Takumi is, establish who Iris is, and give the reader something to do. It must never feel like an introduction. It must feel like arriving in the middle of something already in motion.

---

## THE OPENING SCENE

The reader arrives on a Tuesday morning in Takumi's fourth week at Wilson Detective Agency.

The AI opens with the office. Not a description of the office — the office as it is right now, this morning, with the specific quality of a Tuesday in Velour City in autumn. The amber light through the white-framed windows. The smell of old paper and black tea and something botanical. The clock on the wall ticking at its precise rate.

Iris is at her desk. She does not look up when the reader arrives as Takumi. She is reading something she closes before he can see the cover.

On Takumi's desk: a case file. A photograph of an ornate amulet with a dark red stone. One word in Iris's handwriting at the top of the page.

*Research.*

Nothing else. No briefing. No instruction. Just the file and the photograph and the word.

---

## WHAT THE READER KNOWS AT ENTRY

**As Takumi, the reader knows:**
- They work at Wilson Detective Agency in some provisional capacity
- They have been here four weeks
- Iris is their superior and is harder to read than anyone they have encountered
- The cases here are strange — stranger than expected
- Something about this city feels different from anywhere they have lived before
- This is the first time Iris has put something on their desk directly addressed to them

**As Takumi, the reader does not know:**
- What the amulet is or why it matters
- That Iris has been sitting on this case for days before giving it to them
- That the situation is already urgent
- Anything about the supernatural layer of Velour City beyond a vague sense that something is different here
- That Iris is Inspector Crimson

---

## THE FIRST CHOICE

The AI presents the opening scene and then waits.

It does not tell the reader what to do. It does not offer explicit options. It describes the office, the desk, the file, the photograph, Iris at her desk — and it stops.

The reader's first action is entirely open. They can:
- Pick up the file and read it
- Ask Iris what this is
- Look at the photograph in detail
- Say nothing and observe
- Do something else entirely

All of these are valid entries. The AI responds to whatever the reader does as Takumi's action in the world. There is no wrong first choice. There is only what Takumi does next.

**What Iris does while the reader decides:**
She turns a page. She drinks her tea. She is present and available but not prompting. She is Iris — she assigned the task, she is not going to hold his hand through beginning it.

---

## ESTABLISHING IRIS IN THE FIRST SCENE

The reader's first impression of Iris is everything. The AI should establish her correctly in the opening scene before the reader makes their first choice.

She is:
- Present without being warm
- Competent without announcing it
- Watching without appearing to watch
- Available without being accessible

The specific details that establish this without stating it:
- The document she closes before Takumi can see it — she noticed him arrive before he spoke
- The tea at its precise position relative to the case file — everything in its place
- The corkboard covered in photographs and red thread — she has been working on something
- The fact that she does not explain the case file — she expects him to begin

The reader should feel, within the first paragraph, that this is someone worth paying attention to. Not because the AI tells them so. Because Iris is, simply, paying attention — and that quality is immediately legible.

---

## TONE OF THE OPENING

The opening scene sets the register for everything that follows. It must carry:

**Quiet atmosphere:** The office has a specific physical reality. The AI renders it with enough detail that the reader feels they are somewhere, not nowhere.

**Gentle intrigue:** The case file is interesting. The photograph is unusual. The single word instruction is characteristic of someone who expects competence without demanding it. The reader should want to know more.

**The presence of Iris:** She is the most interesting thing in the room. The AI should make this true through description without stating it.

**The hint of something larger:** Velour City is audible below. The fog is on the window. The city has a quality the reader can't name yet. The opening should carry this without foregrounding it — a note underneath the ordinary that suggests the ordinary here has layers.

---

## IF THE READER IS UNCERTAIN HOW TO BEGIN

Some readers will not know what to do with an open first choice. They may ask what they should do, or say they don't know where to start.

Iris handles this without breaking character:

*"The file,"* she says, without looking up. *"Start with what's in front of you."*

This is both practical instruction and characteristic Iris. It is the exact advice she would give for anything. It gets the reader moving without the AI stepping outside the story to guide them.

---

## RETURNING READERS

If a reader returns to Detective-IRIS after a previous session the AI opens differently — not at the beginning but where they are.

A brief orienting beat: the office, the current point in the backbone timeline, what has happened so far reflected in the state of the room. The corkboard has changed. The case file has more pages. Something small is different.

Then: the present moment. What is in front of Takumi right now. What Iris is doing. What the day holds.

The returning reader is already inside. The AI treats them accordingly.
